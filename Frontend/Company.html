We started FUNDBEE with one belief: that access to credit should not be a privilege. Millions of Indians are underserved by traditional banks — we're here to change that with technology, empathy, and speed.

Our Journey

A Decade of Making
Credit Accessible

2014
FUNDBEE Founded
Incorporated in Mumbai as an NBFC with a vision to democratise lending for urban and semi-urban India.
2016
RBI Registration
Received official NBFC licence from the Reserve Bank of India, cementing our regulatory standing.
2018
Digital Platform Launch
Launched India's first fully digital personal loan app with end-to-end paperless processing and same-day approvals.
2020
₹100 Crore Milestone
Crossed ₹100 Crore in total loan disbursals, serving customers across 12 Indian states.
2022
Business Loans Launched
Expanded our product suite to include SME business loans, helping thousands of entrepreneurs grow their ventures.
2024
₹500 Crore & 25,000 Customers
Reached landmark milestones with over ₹500 Crore disbursed and 25,000 satisfied customers across India.
